Rings

    Rings 1…n−1 are the unknotted base rings. The highest-numbered ring is the word ring — its path is built from the word below.

    Algebraic word

    Type a…z or ring numbers 1 2 3. An apostrophe ' inverts the letter before it (a' = a⁻¹). ^-1 works too. Geometry rebuilds on every keystroke.

    Simulation

    Hold Space to pull. With the cut tool armed, click any ring to snip it; click a snipped ring again to rejoin.
